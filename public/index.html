How it works

1

Share one link

Paste it in your group chat. Friends tap it and join with a nickname. 5 to 30 players.

2

Secret roles

Turncoat deals 1 to 3 players a secret turncoat role. Only you see your own card.

3

Night

The turncoats pick a loyal to take out, on a page only they can open. They have their own secret chat.

4

Day

Argue it out in your chat, then everyone votes. The player with the most votes is banished and their role revealed.

The loyals win when every turncoat is banished. The turncoats win when they match the loyals in number. At the end everyone sees who was who, night by night.

Questions

Does everyone need to be online at the same time?

No. With "one round a day" people vote whenever they like during the day. The game moves on at 8 am and 8 pm in your time zone, or as soon as everyone has voted.

Which chat apps does it work with?

Any of them: WhatsApp, iMessage, Messenger, Signal, Telegram, Slack, Teams, Discord. Turncoat is a web page, so the link opens in any browser on a phone or computer.

Can anyone peek at the roles?

No. Roles are dealt and kept on the server. Your phone only ever receives your own role, and the turncoats' names only if you are one.

How many turncoats are there?

One for 5 to 7 players, two for 8 to 11, three for 12 or more.

Is it free?

Yes. No ads, no account, no app. Games are deleted 60 days after the last move.
